TITLE
Marie Antoinette: The Last Queen of Versailles | Biography for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
Fall asleep to the true story of Marie Antoinette, the little archduchess from Vienna who was sent to France at fourteen, became Queen at eighteen, and is still remembered for words she almost certainly never said. In this calm, slow biography we follow her from a November birth in the Hofburg in 1755, through an imperial childhood at Schönbrunn, the long road to France and the handover on an island in the Rhine, the rules of Versailles, the small house and the thatched village she loved at the Petit Trianon, motherhood and the portraits of Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun, the diamond necklace she never wanted, and the year 1789. Then, gently, the Tuileries, the night journey to Varennes, and her last autumn in 1793. About 135 minutes of gentle narration over a quiet fireside sound, with calm animated chapter cards and two journey maps.
