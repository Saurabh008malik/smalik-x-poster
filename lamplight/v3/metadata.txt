DESCRIPTION
Settle in, dim the lights, and listen to the story of the Exodus: a baby in a basket among the reeds, a shepherd at a bush that burned but was not consumed, "Let my people go", the night of the Passover, a path through the sea, bread from heaven in the wilderness, and the ten words at Mount Sinai. A calm, slow-paced retelling of the book of Exodus for sleep, about two hours long, with the key passages read from scripture itself.

🌙 Best with a low volume and the screen dimmed. You can let it play through the night.

ABOUT THIS VIDEO
The narration is AI-generated (an open-source text-to-speech voice) and is disclosed as altered or synthetic content. The retelling was written for this channel and checked against the text of Exodus. Where the text is hard (the plagues, the night of the Passover, the golden calf), the narration stays honest and gentle; nothing is invented and no sermon is added.

SCRIPTURE
Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (public domain), lightly adapted for reading aloud: the divine name is rendered as "the Lord".
