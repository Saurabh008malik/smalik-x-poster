DESCRIPTION
Settle in, dim the lights, and listen to the opening of the Bible told slowly and gently: the seven days of creation, the garden of Eden, Cain and Abel, Noah and the flood, the tower of Babel, and the long road of Abraham and Sarah, from the call in Haran to the well where Rebekah drew water. A calm, slow-paced retelling of Genesis 1 to 25 for sleep, over two hours long, with the key passages read from scripture itself.
