Marcus Aurelius: The Emperor Who Wrote to Himself | Biography for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
Fall asleep to the true story of Marcus Aurelius, the Roman emperor who wrote the Meditations. In this calm, slow biography we follow him from a quiet childhood on the Caelian Hill in Rome in 121 AD, through his teachers, his letters to Fronto, his marriage to Faustina and the peaceful years beside Antoninus Pius, to two decades on the throne: floods, plague, the long winters by the Danube where he wrote his private notebook by lamplight, the burned letters of a rebel, Athens, and his last days in 180 AD. About 143 minutes of gentle narration over a quiet fireside sound.
