Abraham Lincoln: From Log Cabin to the White House | Biography for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
Fall asleep to the true story of Abraham Lincoln, the sixteenth President of the United States. In this calm, slow biography we follow him from a one-room log cabin by a spring in Kentucky in 1809, through his boyhood in the Indiana woods, a flatboat journey down the Mississippi, the little village of New Salem and the prairie law circuit, to Springfield, the White House, the Civil War years, Gettysburg, and the autumn of 1863, when he asked the whole nation to keep a day of Thanksgiving on the last Thursday of November. About 153 minutes of gentle narration over a quiet fireside sound.
