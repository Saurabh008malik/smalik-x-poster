TITLE
Jane Austen: A Quiet Life of Ink and Wit | Biography for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
Fall asleep to the true story of Jane Austen, the clergyman's daughter who wrote Pride and Prejudice, Emma and Persuasion at a small table in a Hampshire cottage. In this calm, slow biography we follow her from a snowy winter birth at Steventon rectory in 1775, through the family plays in the barn, her teenage notebooks, country dances and Tom Lefroy, a first novel turned down unread, the years in Bath and Southampton with her sailor brothers, the quiet home at Chawton where her books finally went out into the world "By a Lady", a visit to the Prince Regent's library, and her last days in Winchester in 1817. About 138 minutes of gentle narration over a quiet fireside sound.
