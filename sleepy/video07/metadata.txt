Charles Dickens and the Story of A Christmas Carol | Biography for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
Fall asleep to the true story of Charles Dickens, the boy from the blacking warehouse who grew up to write Oliver Twist, David Copperfield, Great Expectations and, in one cold autumn, A Christmas Carol. In this calm, slow biography we follow him from a winter birth in Portsmouth in 1812, through golden childhood years by the Medway, a hard year tying up pots of boot blacking while his father sat in the Marshalsea debtors' prison, shorthand and the reporters' gallery, the first sketch dropped into a dark letter-box, the sudden fame of Mr. Pickwick, a stormy crossing to America, and the six weeks of night walks in which he wrote A Christmas Carol. Then the busy middle years, the real shadows of his marriage, the public readings, Gad's Hill, and a summer evening in 1870. About 147 minutes of gentle narration over a quiet fireside sound.
