# Leonardo da Vinci: research brief (video 2)

Working title: Fall Asleep to the Life of Leonardo da Vinci | Biography for Sleep
Length target: about 115 minutes (about 12,500 words at about 110 wpm)

## Chapters (12)
1. Welcome, and the boy from Vinci: born Saturday 15 April 1452, about three in the night (note by grandfather Antonio); illegitimate son of notary Ser Piero; mother Caterina (identity debated); grandfather Antonio, uncle Francesco; tax record 1457.
2. Looking at the world: Tuscan hills, informal schooling (reading, writing, abacus; no Latin classes), watching water, birds, rocks; father takes him to Florence.
3. Verrocchio's workshop: in Florence by about 1466 to 1469; the bottega crafts; Baptism of Christ (angel, landscape; Vasari's story about Verrocchio is probably a legend); 1472 admitted to the painters' company of St Luke; drawing of the Arno valley dated 5 Aug 1473; Annunciation; Ginevra de' Benci.
4. A young master: stays in the workshop to late 1470s; Adoration of the Magi commissioned 1481 (monks of San Donato at Scopeto), left unfinished; St Jerome unfinished.
5. The court of Milan: about 1482 to Ludovico Sforza ("il Moro"); the letter (mostly military engineering, last paragraph art; draft in Codex Atlanticus); Virgin of the Rocks commission 25 Apr 1483 (De Predis brothers; dispute to 1508); Lady with an Ermine, pageants and stage designs.
6. The notebooks: mirror writing (left-handed, practical), Codex Atlanticus (1,119 leaves), method (observation, "experience"), Vitruvian Man about 1490, birds and flying studies, machines mostly unbuilt; Salaì joins 22 July 1490 (aged ten); Caterina arrives July 1493 and dies within the year (Leonardo pays her funeral).
7. The horse and the Last Supper: clay horse model shown Nov 1493 (wedding of Bianca Maria Sforza to Maximilian); bronze sent for cannon 1494; Last Supper painted 1495 to 1498 at Santa Maria delle Grazie, dry wall (not true fresco), began to flake early; 1652 doorway; 1943 bombing, wall protected by sandbags; restoration 1978 to 1999.
8. Wanderings: 1499 French invade Milan, he leaves; clay horse destroyed; Mantua (drawing of Isabella d'Este), Venice; 1502 military engineer to Cesare Borgia in Romagna; Imola town map.
9. Florence again: 1503 commission for Battle of Anghiari in Palazzo Vecchio, unfinished and lost; 1504 asked for opinion on placing Michelangelo's David; father dies 9 July 1504; begins portrait of Lisa Gherardini (wife of Francesco del Giocondo) about 1503; St Anne.
10. The anatomist: old man at Santa Maria Nuova (about 1507/8); about thirty dissections; Marcantonio della Torre at Pavia 1510 to 11; wax in the brain; heart as pump; never published; Milan 1508 to 1513 under governor Charles d'Amboise.
11. Rome and the road to France: Sept/end 1513 to Rome (Belvedere rooms, Giuliano de' Medici, few commissions); Francis I invites him; leaves Italy end of 1516; Cloux near Amboise; "first painter, architect and engineer to the king"; brings Mona Lisa, St Anne, St John.
12. Cloux, and goodnight: will 23 April 1519 (Melzi executor and heir of papers; Salaì half a garden); died 2 May 1519 aged 67; buried at Saint-Florentin; church later demolished, grave unknown; Francis I acquired the Mona Lisa (1518 per Louvre); legacy.

## Facts to treat with care
- Birthplace: Vinci or the hamlet of Anchiano; say "in or near Vinci".
- Caterina: identity debated (Kemp and Pallanti propose Caterina di Meo Lippi, a young orphan; others suggested an enslaved woman from abroad); say "little is certainly known".
- Apprenticeship start: mid-teens, between about 1466 and 1469.
- Milan arrival: about 1482 (Museo Galileo); one Britannica summary says 1492, which is an error.
- Vasari anecdotes (Verrocchio giving up painting; Francis I holding the dying Leonardo's head) are later stories: frame as "it is said".
- Horse model destruction: "French soldiers", soldiers' target practice is a tradition; keep soft.
- Last Supper completion: 1497 or 1498.
- Do not state the Mona Lisa sitter as certain beyond "traditionally and by the Louvre: Lisa Gherardini".
- Leonardo's own words: paraphrase only, no invented quotes.
