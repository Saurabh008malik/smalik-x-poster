# Viral patterns in our niche — Cricket Ki Baat (Hindi cricket news)
Source: vidIQ outlier search, Instagram Reels + TikTok, Oct 2026. These got 3x–500x MORE views than the creator's normal.
Copy the PATTERN (hook type, structure, format). Never copy their words. Facts must come from fresh research.

1. **Record / stat news card** — bold Hinglish headline "Kohli ka record todkar Abhishek bane naye T20 shatakveer" + 3–4 big stats, slow zoom, music only. (4.2M views, 13.5x) ← exactly our card/reel style, use it daily for the biggest performance.
2. **Curiosity question about a viral moment** — "Stadium mein kya hua? 😳", "Ye dekh ke sab hans pade". Story told in 3 beats. (2.3M)
3. **Last-over drama** — "7 gend, 17 run — poora drama 🥵" ball-by-ball countdown of the finish. (2.5M)
4. **Comeback / team news** — "Bhuvi ki wapsi! 🔥", "Monsters are back" — selection, injury, comeback. (908K, 47x; 830K, 22x)
5. **Funny one-liner reaction headline** — "NZ be like: humko ghar jaana hai 😂" over the result. (1M)
6. **Debate question** — "Kya MI comeback karegi? Comment karo 👇", "Ye squad sahi hai?" — invites comments. (830K)
7. **Emotional quote on a big moment** — match moment + a life-lesson line in Hindi. (2M, 519x)

Hooks that win: player name + record/number, "kya hua?", "wapsi", funny "be like", a direct question to fans.
Our own data (perf-cricket.md): reels score about 4x more than cards; player-milestone reels (Janssen, Harmer, Erasmus) did best.
