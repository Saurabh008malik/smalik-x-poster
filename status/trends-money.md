# Viral patterns in our niche — Paycheck Truths (US personal finance)
Source: vidIQ outlier search, Instagram Reels + TikTok, Oct 2026. These got 6x–2600x MORE views than the creator's normal.
Copy the PATTERN (hook type, structure, format). Never copy their words. Facts and numbers must come from fresh research.

1. **Two-column sort** — "CASH or FINANCE?" items sorted one by one with a one-line verdict (iPhone, car, house...). (6.2M views, 2644x)
2. **Then vs Now** — "1960 vs 2026": one income vs two incomes, house/car/groceries side by side; hook "Why is everyone so stressed?" (1.8M, 66x)
3. **Big listicle with a dollar promise** — "15 habits that cut $1,000 a month" over calm footage, text only, music only. (1.7M, 66x)
4. **Reassurance list** — "Signs you're doing better than you think" (no credit card debt, 3 months saved...). Relatable, loops. (1.1M, 55x)
5. **"The math nobody says out loud"** — one counter-intuitive money fact with 3–4 numbers, ends with a question. (760K)
6. **Simple formula in steps** — "How to be financially free in 5 steps" / "if essentials eat more than 55%...". (927K, 422x)
7. **"If X happens, say this"** — exact words to say to a debt collector / boss / landlord. (978K, 214x)
8. **Paycheck relatability** — "This is 10 hours of someone's life 😳", "week 2 of biweekly pay". (1.1M, 630x)

Hooks that win: a big number or dollar amount, "nobody tells you", "signs you're...", "say this", then-vs-now.
