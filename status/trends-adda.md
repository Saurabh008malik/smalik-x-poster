# Viral patterns in our niche — Creator Adda (Hindi YouTube tips)
Source: vidIQ outlier search, Instagram Reels + TikTok, Oct 2026. These reels got 5x–230x MORE views than their creator's normal.
Copy the PATTERN (hook type, structure, format). Never copy their words or claims. Facts must still come from fresh research.

1. **Bold promise + steps** — hook "100% views aayenge, bas ye karo" → 3–4 numbered steps → "nahi aaye to comment karna". (826K views, 27x)
2. **"Sahi tarika" tutorial of one YouTube Studio feature** — "YT Studio khud bata raha kya video banao 🔥", "Naya channel monetize karne ka sahi tarika". One feature, step by step, phone screenshots style. (970K and 837K views)
3. **Numbered series** — "Day 1 of YouTube Series", faceless big text on screen + beat, fast cuts, ends "Day 2 kal". (716K, 39x) → run a running series (Day 1, Day 2...) on reels.
4. **"3 cheezein / 3 shots" numbered demo** — big number 1, 2, 3 on screen, each with a quick example. (2.9M, 25x)
5. **One framework** — "YouTube crack karne ka sirf ek formula: Topic → Title → Thumbnail → Script". (1.3M, 233x)
6. **Start-now motivation** — "Aaj shuru nahi kiya to bahut der ho jayegi", short, emotional. (837K)
7. **Earning case study** — "English video ko Hindi mein bana ke ye channel kama raha hai" with simple numbers. (831K)

Hooks that win: a promise with a number ("100%", "3 cheezein", "sirf 1 formula"), "sahi tarika", "galti mat karna", Myth vs Fact.
Our own top performers (see perf-adda.md): Myth vs Fact reels and "pehle 30 second mein ye 4 cheezen karo" cards.
