TITLE
The Story of Joseph: Fall Asleep to the Whole Story | Bible Stories for Sleep

DESCRIPTION
Settle in, dim the lights, and listen to one of the best-loved stories ever told: Joseph, the dreamer in the coat of many colors, thrown into a pit by his brothers, sold into Egypt, forgotten in a prison, and raised up to stand beside Pharaoh. A calm, slow-paced retelling of Genesis 37 to 50 for sleep, over two hours long, from the dreams of the sheaves to the words "you meant evil against me, but God meant it for good", with the key passages read from scripture itself.

🌙 Best with a low volume and the screen dimmed. You can let it play through the night.
